My MSc thesis estimated blood glucose from PPG with a hybrid CNN-GRU network. Along the way I have worked with ECG, BCG and radar signals, taught computer vision and machine learning to more than 200 students, and learned to care most about models that hold up on unseen subjects, unseen datasets and poor lighting, not only on average.

Research

rPPG · Radar vital signs · Biosignal deep learning

My research asks how far we can go in measuring physiology without contact or with a single cheap sensor: heart rate from a camera or a radar, glucose from a PPG pulse, stress from a wearable. The application I focus on now is driver monitoring, where light changes fast, the head moves, and the model has to work for people it has never seen.

Key research directions

  • A) Multimodal contactless heart rate for drivers: fusing RGB video, NIR video and FMCW radar so each sensor covers the others' weak conditions, with self-supervised pretraining and fusion architectures as the main levers.
  • B) rPPG under extreme lighting: keeping pulse recovery reliable at night and in mixed light, and classifying illumination in rPPG video into five lux levels (L0–L4) without labels, for dataset curation and quality control.
  • C) Deep learning for biosignals in health: non-invasive blood glucose from PPG, ECG–PPG stress classification, and blood pressure estimation next.

How I evaluate

  • A) Subject-independent splits only (leave-one-subject-out or subject-wise folds), so no person appears in both training and test.
  • B) Cross-dataset testing, with the same preprocessing and metrics for every method.
  • C) Worst-condition error reported next to the average, because a driver-monitoring model is only as good as its darkest, shakiest minute.

Goals for the PhD

  • A) Publications in IEEE Transactions and Q1 journals, plus top vision venues.
  • B) An open benchmark toolbox for radar vital signs, in the spirit of rPPG-Toolbox.
  • C) Reproducible code and fair comparisons that other groups can build on.

Heart rate from RGB and near-infrared face video, with a focus on low light, mixed illumination and head motion inside the car.

Radar vital signs

FMCW radar processing (clutter removal, CFAR detection, target localization) feeding multi-scale CNNs for contactless heart rate.

Biosignal deep learning

Hybrid and transfer-learning models on PPG and ECG: non-invasive glucose estimation and end-to-end stress classification.

What I am working toward

  • Heart rate that stays accurate for drivers at night and on the move.
  • Fusion of camera and radar that degrades gracefully when one sensor fails.
  • Fair, cross-dataset benchmarks for rPPG and radar vital signs.
  • Label-free quality checks for large video datasets.
  • Wearable-grade models for glucose, stress and blood pressure.
  • Open code that other labs can reproduce.
